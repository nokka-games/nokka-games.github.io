Studio

Nokka is Finnish for beak.

That's our N: two interlocking hooks whose diagonals taper into beaks. It's also why paper birds keep drifting across the top of this page.

We're a small, independent studio. We'd rather make one game that feels great than several that feel fine, and we sweat the details you only notice when they're missing.

  • Small on purpose. Tight scope, finished properly.
  • Feel first. Every button, sound and number tuned by hand.
  • Fair by design. No dark patterns, no pay-to-skip.
