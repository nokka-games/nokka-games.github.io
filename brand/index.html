Accent

The mark is always one color: Ink, Paper or white. Accent is for small highlights such as links and selections, never for the mark.

Usage

  • Clear space: keep at least one stem width empty on every side of the mark.
  • Minimum size: 16px for the mark, 120px wide for the horizontal lockup.
  • Keep it as drawn: don't stretch, rotate, outline, add effects or rebuild it from a font.
